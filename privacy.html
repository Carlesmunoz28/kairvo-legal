Kairvo Video Studio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Kairvo Video Studio ("the app") is a Windows desktop application. It has no servers of its own and does not collect, sell or share personal data.

What the app uses from TikTok

  • When you connect TikTok, the app asks only for user.info.basic (your account ID and display name, to show which account is connected) and video.upload (to send your videos to TikTok as drafts).
  • The access tokens TikTok returns are stored only on your computer, encrypted with Windows (DPAPI). They are never sent anywhere except to TikTok's own API.
  • The app does not read your followers, messages, likes or any other TikTok data.

Your control

  • You can disconnect TikTok at any time from the app, which deletes the stored tokens, or revoke access from your TikTok settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all its local data.
